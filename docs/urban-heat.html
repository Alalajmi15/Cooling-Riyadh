Urban Heat in Riyadh

Summer heat is not distributed evenly across Riyadh. Using Landsat Collection 2 Level-2 imagery, this section maps land surface temperature across the study area and highlights where the urban surface is relatively hotter or cooler. These thermal patterns provide the basis for identifying areas where heat exposure may overlap with limited vegetation, population concentration, and dense development.

Explore Land Surface Temperature

Land surface temperature (LST) represents the temperature of the ground and built surfaces observed by the satellite rather than air temperature. Across the final 500 m analysis grid, mean LST is approximately 51.1°C, with grid-cell averages ranging from about 40.7°C to 57.7°C.
